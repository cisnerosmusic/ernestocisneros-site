Privacy Policy

Last updated — April 7, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Ernesto Cisneros Cino ("I", "me", or "my") handles information in connection with the mobile applications published under my Google Play developer account and the website ernestocisneros.art.

Information Collection

My applications and website are designed with privacy in mind. I do not collect, store, or share any personal information, including but not limited to:

  • Names, email addresses, or phone numbers
  • Location data
  • Device identifiers or advertising IDs
  • Financial or payment information
  • Contacts, photos, or other files on your device

Analytics

The website ernestocisneros.art uses GoatCounter, a privacy-friendly, open-source analytics service that does not use cookies, does not collect personal data, and is fully compliant with GDPR, CCPA, and PECR. No personal information is gathered or transmitted through this service.

My mobile applications do not include any analytics, tracking SDKs, or third-party data collection services.

Third-Party Services

My applications may link to external services such as streaming platforms, social media, or informational websites. These third-party services operate under their own privacy policies, and I am not responsible for their content or data practices. I encourage you to review the privacy policies of any third-party service you access through my applications.

Data Storage & Security

Since no personal data is collected, there is no personal data stored on any server or database under my control. Any data generated by the applications (such as user preferences or local settings) remains solely on your device and is never transmitted externally.

Children's Privacy

My applications do not knowingly collect any information from children under the age of 13. Since no personal data is collected from any user, my applications are safe for use by individuals of all ages.

Changes to This Policy

I may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated revision date. Continued use of my applications or website after such changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
