ABOUT ERNESTO CISNEROS CINO:

Born in Havana, Cuba. Currently based in Miami, Florida (since 2024). He has been studying and creating music since the age of six, with a career spanning over 30 years across composition, production, live performance, digital art, literature, and education. He has over 800 registered works across music, visual art, and literature.

EDUCATION:
- Classical piano studies from age six in Havana
- Instituto Pedagógico Superior Enrique José Varona, Havana: Primary Education (completed in the 1990s), Artistic Education (2000-2004)
- One year immersed in art and pedagogy in Mexico (1999)

MUSIC CAREER:
- 1987: Joined Paisaje con Río, a Havana rock-pop band with socially conscious lyrics
- 2000s: Worked extensively in film and television, composing soundtracks for series, telenovelas, documentaries, and cultural programs
- Won Cubadisco awards (Suite Habana)
- Genres: jazz, electroacoustic, symphonic, rock, ethnic music, concert music
- Philosophy: "Music taught me to listen before speaking, to structure emotion, and to find beauty in the tension between order and chaos."

ALBUMS:
1. Glacial Paths: Ten pieces for piano and synthesizers, composed in the space between silence and sound. Contemplative instrumental pieces navigating frozen landscapes.
2. Atlas of Fragmented Light: Eleven tracks mapping the cartography of sound and memory.
3. Mare Incognitum: A sonic manifesto of unstable cartography. Ten pieces exploring unknown territories of sound.
4. Trash: Seven pieces born from cinema that never reached the screen. Now an NFT collection on Tezos.
5. Sandbank: Collaborative NFT project with Gino Battiston. Documentary film of the Patagonian desert of Mendoza, Argentina.
6. Other Musical Works: Archive of film soundtracks, TV compositions, and experimental works from 30+ years.

KEY COLLABORATORS:

NFT AND WEB3:
Ernesto entered the NFT space in 2020 during the COVID-19 pandemic, through a small network of Cuban creators. He views blockchain not as financial speculation but as a new cultural infrastructure where artists retain ownership, smart contracts enable sovereign distribution, global collectors support creators without intermediaries, and communities form organically across languages and cultures.

He was active in the Clubhouse era (2020-2021), participating in open discussions about smart contracts, creative methodologies, and cross-disciplinary learning.

He works across three blockchain ecosystems:
- Tezos: First mints on Kalamint (now defunct), works live on objkt.com and artist-controlled FA2 contracts
- Ethereum: OpenSea, Foundation (during invitation-only period), community drops, independent smart contract deployments
- Bitcoin: Bitcoin Ordinals

Notable NFT works include: The Bride of the Moon (with SPHYNX, 0.888 ETH), Canto del Cello (with ojovivo, 1.02 ETH, exhibited at Lynn University NFT Museum 2023), The Veil (with ojovivo, 1.5 ETH), Mother (with ojovivo, 1.17 ETH), Seafarers (with Kevin Oramas, 0.90 ETH), Heart Strings (with Mariana Blatnik, 0.40 ETH), Arhat (independent smart contract, pure audio composition stored on Arweave), Discordant (first NFT ever minted, May 10, 2021).

IMPULSES PROJECT:
A multi-decade initiative. Since the 2000s: mentoring young musicians and composers in Cuba. 2007: Participation in "El Rostro de los Días" (innovative telenovela). 2020-2023: 20 NFT artists in European galleries. 2024: Musical therapy for migrant communities in Miami through impulses.art.

PHILOSOPHY ON EXILE:
"Exile is not a place; it is a frequency. A vibration that reorganizes the mind, reshapes memory, and forces identity to breathe in a different rhythm." Ernesto sees exile as both a wound and a creative engine, a point of view that sharpens artistic expression.
