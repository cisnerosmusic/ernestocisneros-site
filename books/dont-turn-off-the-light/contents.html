Don't Turn Off the Light

Eleven horror stories

«Almost everything that truly frightens waits for you to go still.…»

Read the prologue →

The stories

I
The Man from the South
A photographer discovers a man who disappeared twenty-two years ago, identical, unaged.
II
The Hollow
A door behind a wardrobe. A room Marta doesn't remember. A childhood that wasn't what she thought.
III
The Weight
An office worker gains weight without explanation. The weight grows. Something beneath the skin begins to move.
IV
The Call
A phone rings at dawn. A voice that shouldn't exist asks for help from a place that shouldn't be.
V
The Passenger
A man gets into a taxi and gives an address. The driver knows it. Because it's his own.
VI
The List
A man inherits a list with six names crossed out. The seventh name is his.
VII
The Silence
A music teacher loses her ability to hear. In its place, she hears something else.
VIII
The Book That Rewrites Itself
A writer discovers his novel changes on its own. The characters know things he never gave them.
IX
The Perfect Crowd
A hundred people react in unison. They laugh, cry, look at the camera at the same moment.
X
The Forgiveness
A father comes home from work. His daughter asks forgiveness for something he doesn't know happened.
XI
The Visitor
An old man receives a visit from someone who claims to be him, forty years younger.
